Privacy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

The short version

Creation Generation has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no ads today. The console at /play runs entirely in your browser and never uploads your files.

The console (/play)

  • Your files stay with you. On the From Existing screen, files you pick are listed by name, type and size only. They are never read into the page or uploaded anywhere.
  • Preferences stay in your browser. Sound, captions, mode, spend cap, creativity XP and your walkthrough progress are saved in your browser's local storage (keys starting with cg4.). Clear your browser's site data to remove them.
  • Clipboard only when you press a button. Copy and Send buttons put a command on your clipboard so you can paste it into your AI chat. The page never reads your clipboard.
  • No ads and no trackers on the console, ever.

Who else is involved

  • Hosting: this site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security and operations; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts: pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads; see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Your AI chat: what you paste or upload into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or any other chat app is governed by that app's own privacy policy, not this one.

Advertising

There is no advertising on this site today. If ads are added to the home page later, this policy will be updated first to say who serves them, what cookies they use and how to opt out. Ads will never appear inside the console.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Questions

Does it cost money?

No. The cartridge is free, and the spend cap starts at $0, so only free tools run. Anything paid shows a quote and waits for your yes.

Do I need to code?

No. Everything works in a normal AI chat. If your chat app can run code, the built-in quality checks run for real.

Do I have to type commands?

No. The console builds the command for you. Press Send, paste it in your chat and press Enter. AVANI carries on from there.

Can I bring my own scripts, chats and art?

Yes. Choose From Existing (or type /import). Your words stay word for word, and anything that conflicts is flagged for you.

How do I continue tomorrow, or in another app?

Type /save and keep the block it prints. In any new chat, load the cartridge, type /load and paste the block.

Is my work private?

The console runs in your browser. Files you pick are listed by name, type and size only and are never uploaded. Your work lives in your own AI chat. See the privacy policy.
